How the model works

Arrivals are Poisson (exponential time between arrivals, mean 2 minutes by default). Service times are triangular in minutes: check-in (1, 2, 3), vitals and nurse assessment (3, 4, 5), doctor assessment (5, 10, 15), procedure (10, 12.5, 15), check-out (1, 2, 3).

The flow follows the Simio file: Check_in, then Waiting_room, where patients wait for a nurse, then Nurse_assessment, then ExamRoom_Dr. A patient waits in the exam room's line for a free room, keeps the room while waiting for a doctor, and sees the doctor there. Patients on Path5 then go to the procedure waiting room, wait in line for a procedure room, and keep the room while waiting for a doctor. Patients on Path6 go straight to check-out. Doctors are one shared pool for exam and procedure rooms, first come, first served.

The saved results inside the Simio file show the doctor exam at ExamRoom_Dr averaging about 10 seconds, with a range of about 5 to 14 seconds. That matches Triangular(5, 10, 15) in seconds, while Table 1 of the assignment lists it in minutes. The "Doctor exam time" setting lets you run either version. Seconds reproduces the Simio file.

Simio counts a patient who holds a room but is still waiting for the doctor in that room's input buffer. The exam room and procedure room waiting numbers here are counted the same way, so they line up with Simio's InputBuffer columns.

The clinic opens empty, admits patients for the full day length, and stops the clock at closing. Throughput counts patients who finished check-out by closing. Time in system and average waits count those patients. When the clinic is overloaded, many patients are still inside at closing, so watch that number alongside time in system. Utilization and average queue lengths are time averages over the day.

Each patient's service times and procedure draw come from that patient's own random stream, and replication k uses the same seed in every configuration (common random numbers). This makes differences between configurations reflect the design change more than sampling noise. The ± values are 95% confidence half-widths across replications.

The Simio model logic is encoded, so this rebuild is based on the object names, buffers and statistics saved in the file. The default 24-hour day matches the length of Simio's saved run. Travel time on Simio's paths is a few seconds and is left out.
